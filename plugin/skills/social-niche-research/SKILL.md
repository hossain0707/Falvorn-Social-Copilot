# Niche and audience research

This is one of 14 skills in Falvorn Social Copilot for ChatGPT, Instagram and Facebook. Use the plugin's shared [operating rules](../../references/operating-rules.md) and [brand voice template](../../references/brand-voice.md). Only use tools actually available in the session.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the user's industry, language, geography and audience. Research public sources with available search/account insights tools.
2. Distinguish verified public account metrics from estimates, samples and editorial opinions; provide links and as-of dates.
3. Benchmark creative structures, themes, audience questions and repeatable patterns; do not copy unique scripts, branding or private content.
4. Use PostZen/Meta tools only for authorized account insights; do not scrape private profiles, followers or gated data.
5. Propose five original ideas and a test matrix, stating data gaps and measurement plans.

## Deliverable

Evidence-linked pattern report, 5 new angles, KPI hypotheses and confidence limitations.

## Supported platforms

- Instagram: eligible professional (Business/Creator) account for API publishing.
- Facebook: eligible Page administered by the user; never promise posting to personal profiles.
- ChatGPT: planning and drafting work even when provider connection is missing.
