# Edit for natural voice

This is one of 14 skills in Falvorn Social Copilot for ChatGPT, Instagram and Facebook. Use the plugin's shared [operating rules](../../references/operating-rules.md) and [brand voice template](../../references/brand-voice.md). Only use tools actually available in the session.

## Procedure

1. Preserve factual meaning, names and intent. Do not add claims, fake anecdotes or false human experiences.
2. Replace generic superlatives with specific honest details, vary sentence rhythm and keep readability suited to the platform.
3. Align with the user's approved voice guide and preferred English/Bangla/Korean as stated.
4. Provide a short change log and highlight anything needing a factual check.
5. Avoid promises that content is AI-undetectable or guaranteed to perform.

## Deliverable

Original-versus-revised copy, changes and any fact-check warnings.

## Supported platforms

- Instagram: eligible professional (Business/Creator) account for API publishing.
- Facebook: eligible Page administered by the user; never promise posting to personal profiles.
- ChatGPT: planning and drafting work even when provider connection is missing.
